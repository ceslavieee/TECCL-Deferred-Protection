# InterDC8 three-load admission evidence

This directory is generated by
`teccl/examples/plot_dynamic_admission_multiload.py` from the predeclared
endpoint reports and the independently validated 16-seed transition-load
summary.

| lambda | offered arrivals / deadline | early DPP A/B/U | DDPP A/B/U | paired DDPP W/T/L |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

The endpoint loads use eight formal seeds each. The transition load uses 16
held-out seeds. Seed `20260828` was an excluded engineering integrity gate.
Every accepted endpoint schedule passes the independent strict-tree and
failure-time audits; every blocked endpoint request is solver-proved
`INFEASIBLE`; the sole endpoint unknown is a time-limited solve with no
incumbent and no ledger mutation.

Panel (a) reports observed unknown-aware blocking intervals. Panel (b) reports
the mean paired certified-admission difference and 95% paired-mean t interval.
The result supports an InterDC8 load trend under the declared trace family, not
universal dominance across topologies or arrival models.
